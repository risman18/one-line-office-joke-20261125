One line, at most 200 characters: a joke about office life, the sort of line you send a colleague after a meeting that should have been an email.

## Entry

> Facilities replaced our dead office plant with a laminated photo of one, then closed the ticket as 'resolved'.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt`.
